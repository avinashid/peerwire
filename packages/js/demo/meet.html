Start a video call

Browser to browser, peer to peer: no account and no server of ours. Up to 6 people.

  • Audio and video go straight between the browsers, encrypted.
  • Each browser sends its video to every other one, so calls stop at 6.
  • Anyone with the invite link can join: it is the room’s only key.
  • As in any direct call, the others can see your IP addresses, local ones too once the camera or microphone is allowed.
